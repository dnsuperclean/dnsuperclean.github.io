Service 01

Regular Cleaning

The standing appointment that keeps a home consistently clean rather than occasionally rescued. Weekly, every other week or monthly across Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater and the rest of the bay — same standard every visit.

🧽 What's included
  • Kitchen — counters, sink, stovetop, appliance exteriors and backsplash
  • Bathrooms — toilets, tubs, showers, sinks, mirrors and fixtures
  • All floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Dusting of surfaces, shelves, décor and reachable fixtures
  • Beds made and living areas tidied
  • Trash emptied and liners replaced
  • Interior glass, mirrors and high-touch points wiped

Who it's for: busy households, families, condo owners and anyone who'd rather stop thinking about it — homes already in reasonable shape that need keeping that way.

Service 02

Deep Cleaning

The reset. This is the service behind most of our before-and-after photos — grout lines in Treasure Island, shower glass in St. Petersburg, hard-water scale in Clearwater and stainless steel in Tampa.

✨ What's included
  • Everything in a regular clean, plus:
  • Tile and grout scrubbed — floors, showers and backsplashes
  • Soap scum and hard-water build-up removed from glass, tile and fixtures
  • Showerheads, faucets and chrome descaled and polished
  • Stainless steel appliances degreased and buffed
  • Baseboards, door frames, trim and switch plates hand-wiped
  • Blinds, vents, ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted
  • Interior windows, sills and sliding-door tracks
  • Behind and underneath movable furniture

Who it's for: first-time clients, seasonal resets, and any home or rental that hasn't had a professional clean in a while. If you're not sure which service you need, this is usually the right place to start.

Service 03

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Empty-home detail for renters handing back keys and owners preparing a place for whoever's next. Nothing is hidden behind furniture, so nothing gets skipped.

📦 What's included
  • Inside and outside of all cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Inside the oven, refrigerator, microwave and dishwasher
  • All bathrooms scrubbed top to bottom
  • Baseboards, doors, door frames and trim
  • Interior windows, sills and tracks
  • Light fixtures, vents, fans and switch plates
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped and edged
  • Garage, patio or lanai sweep on request

Who it's for: tenants at the end of a lease, landlords turning a unit around, and homeowners preparing for a sale or handover anywhere in the Tampa Bay area.

Service 04

Post-Construction Cleaning

Construction dust travels into everything and settles for weeks. We do the final pass that makes a finished build actually look finished — new construction, renovations and remodels across the Tampa Bay area.

🏗️ What's included
  • Fine construction and drywall dust removed from every surface
  • Stickers, labels, paint spots and adhesive residue removed
  • Cabinets and closets cleaned inside and out
  • Windows, frames, sills and tracks detailed
  • Light fixtures, vents, fans and outlets wiped down
  • Appliances cleaned inside and out
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped and edged — repeat passes where dust keeps settling
  • Leftover debris and packaging cleared out

Who it's for: builders, general contractors, remodelers and homeowners at handover — anyone who needs a job site to become a home again.

Service 05

Airbnb & Vacation Rental Cleaning

Short-term rentals live and die on the review. We turn over units on Clearwater Beach, Treasure Island and in New Port Richey — reset, restocked and staged to match the photos in your listing.

🏝️ What's included
  • Linens and towels stripped, washed and reset
  • Beds made and styled to your listing photos
  • Full kitchen and bathroom reset
  • Restock of the paper goods, soap and amenities you supply
  • Dishes, appliances and trash handled
  • Living areas staged and guest-ready

Who it's for: Airbnb and VRBO hosts, property managers and beach condo owners who need a consistent turnover between guests.

Service 06

Office & Commercial Cleaning

Dependable service for offices, suites and commercial spaces across Tampa, Largo, Clearwater and St. Petersburg — scheduled around your working day rather than through the middle of it. For larger floors we bring machine floor care, not just a mop.

🏢 What's included
  • Desks, workstations and common areas
  • Breakrooms and kitchens — counters, sinks and appliances
  • Restrooms cleaned and restocked
  • Trash and recycling removed
  • Entry glass, interior windows and high-touch points
  • Floors vacuumed, mopped and machine-cleaned where needed
  • Conference rooms reset between meetings

Who it's for: small offices, professional and medical suites, salons, studios, retail units and any commercial space that needs a reliable recurring clean.
